# Overview
Cakey Personal is a subscription for **you**, not for a server. Its perks follow you into every server you share with Cakey Bot, whether or not that server has Premium.

Personal is separate from [Server Premium and Custom Bot](/en/core/setup-premium), and it **stacks** with them: a Personal subscriber in a Premium server gets the server's Premium features *and* their own Personal perks. Personal never replaces a server plan and never gives other members anything.

> Manage everything on your [User Customization](/en/feature/user-customization) page at [cakey.bot/user-customization](https://cakey.bot/user-customization).

## AI Allowance
The server's own monthly limit is always used **first**. Once it runs out, Cakey Bot uses your personal allowance instead, so a subscriber in a free server can make roughly 60 chat requests, 15 image generations and 150 voice transcriptions in a month (the server's 20 / 5 / 50 plus their own 40 / 10 / 100). The server's limit is shared by everybody there; your allowance is yours alone. Your allowance resets on the 1st of every month (UTC), and you can see how much you have used on the [User Customization](/en/feature/user-customization) page.

Custom Bot servers that use their own AI key have no limit, so nothing is taken from your allowance there.

## Leaderboard Colours
A member's row uses the first colour that applies:
1. Cakey Bot staff
2. Custom Bot owner (gold)
3. **Cakey Personal (lime)**
4. Server Premium (purple)

The Personal icon is shown for every subscriber even when a higher colour is used for the row.

# Server Controls
Server admins keep control of their server. On the [web dashboard](https://cakey.bot/dashboard):
* **Leveling > Cakey Personal XP bonus** switches the +50% XP on or off for the server.
* **Leveling > Personal rank cards** decides whether members' own rank card banners are shown, or the server's card is always used.
* **Economy > Cakey Personal coin bonus** switches the +25% coins and the doubled daily/weekly/monthly rewards on or off.
* **Custom Bot > Show Cakey Personal styling on leaderboards** lets Custom Bot owners hide the lime highlight and icon on their bot.

All of these are **on** by default.

# Your Privacy
Leaderboards are public, so your Personal status is only shown if you want it to be. On the [User Customization](/en/feature/user-customization) page you can switch off, separately:
* The lime highlight
* The icon
* Your Personal status on public leaderboard pages
* The Personal tier badge on your rank card

# Managing or Cancelling
1. Go to the [main website](https://cakey.bot) and log in.
2. Open the profile menu in the top-right corner and choose **Manage Subscriptions**.
3. Find your Cakey Personal subscription and cancel it.

When you cancel, your perks keep working until the end of the period you already paid for. Nothing is deleted: your rank card, icon and appearance settings are kept and come back the moment you subscribe again. Lifetime never expires.

Refunds follow the same policy as the other plans: a full refund within the first week, contact us through a ticket on the [Support Discord](https://cakey.bot/discord).

# Frequently Asked Questions
## Does Personal work in a server without Premium?
Yes, that is the point. Your XP and coin bonuses, rank card, leaderboard styling, music perks, fishing rod and AI allowance work in every server you share with Cakey Bot, unless a server admin has switched a bonus off for their server.

## Can I use it on a Custom Bot?
Yes. The perks work on Custom Bots too. Custom Bot owners can hide the Personal leaderboard styling on their bot, and nothing is taken from your AI allowance where the owner uses their own AI key.

## How quickly do perks start?
Within about a minute of a successful payment.

## What if I already have a Server Premium subscription?
They stack. Keep both if you want both: Server Premium upgrades the server, Personal upgrades you.
